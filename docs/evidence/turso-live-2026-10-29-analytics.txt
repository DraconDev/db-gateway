The contrast that matters for the comparison: Turso's Top Queries shows the
operator's LITERAL VALUES in the statement text, because it is the tenant's own
data inside the provider's multi-tenant fleet. `dg queries` shows the statement
SHAPE with literals replaced by `?`.
