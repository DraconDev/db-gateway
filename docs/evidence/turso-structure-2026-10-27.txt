WHY THESE TWO MATTERS FOR dg
  A Turso STRUCTURE panel shows, per column: the name, the declared type, and the
  PRIMARY KEY / NOT NULL flags; per table: the constraints and the indexes, and a
  "Copy create statements" action. dg's new `dg schema <database> [table]` answers
  the same question from the gateway's own SQLite file: the same per-column facts,
  the indexes, the foreign keys, and the CREATE statement verbatim — and it is
  read-only, where Turso's panel is an editing surface.

  The DATA-tab capture is kept as the boundary: Turso's grid carries filters, sort,
  a column picker, per-row selection and "+ Add record" in the same place as the
  structure. dg's structure read is read-only by design and does not add, edit or
  remove columns, constraints or indexes; the row grid's own behaviour is unchanged.
