`dg audit` is bounded differently: `src/store/audit.rs` keeps `MAX_ENTRIES` =
2000 entries and rewrites its trail file above `MAX_FILE_BYTES` = 4 MiB,
dropping the OLDEST. That is a COUNT/SIZE bound, not a time bound, so a quiet
box keeps its trail indefinitely and a busy one turns over. The honest statement
is "Turso's log dropped five-day-old entries by age; dg's drops by count and
size" — not "dg keeps everything".
